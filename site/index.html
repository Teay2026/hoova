I run on your Mac. Fast. Private.

My built-in AI runs on your Mac. It’s Gemma 4, and your text stays there. Same with Ollama, if you already use it.

Or bring your own AI key: OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or Mistral. Then your text goes to the provider you pick.

No account. No tracking.

Open source: read my code, or build me yourself.

Install

  1. 1

    Open Hoova.dmg, then drag my icon to Applications.

  2. 2

    Open it. macOS says it can’t check the app.

  3. 3

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, then Open. Only once.

Why the warning? Apple’s signature costs $99 a year. Hoova is free, and its code is public.

On first launch, you pick my AI. Gemma 4 E4B is a one-time 5.2 GB download, E2B a lighter 3.6 GB. With your own AI key, there’s nothing to download.

Rather build me yourself? See the README.

Why I ask for permissions

Accessibility
To read the word under your cursor, or the text you select.
Screen Recording
Some apps, like Chrome, won’t tell me what’s under your cursor. So I read the screen around it, right on your Mac. The picture is never sent or kept.

I never keep what I read. With my built-in AI or Ollama, nothing leaves your Mac.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no subscription. Hoova is open source, under the MIT license.

Does my text leave my Mac?

Not with my built-in AI or Ollama. With your own key, I send your provider the word, the text around it (up to about 12,000 characters, including the start of the page) and the page title. Never a picture of your screen. Some free plans, Gemini’s for one, let the provider use your text, so check its terms.

Which Macs?

Macs with Apple Silicon, on macOS 27 or later.

What about Windows?

Not yet. Watch the repo on GitHub to hear first.

Can I help?

Yes. Report a bug, share an idea in Discussions, or send a pull request.
