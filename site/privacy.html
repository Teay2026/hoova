Privacy

In short: I run on your Mac, I have no server, and I collect nothing.

Your text

When you tap ⌃ twice, I read the word or sentence you point at, and what’s around it. I never keep it. With my built-in AI or Ollama, it never leaves your Mac.

Your screen

Some apps, like Chrome, won’t tell me what’s under your cursor. So I read the screen around it, right on your Mac. The picture is never sent or kept.

My AI model

On first launch, you pick. Gemma 4 E4B is a one-time 5.2 GB download, E2B a lighter 3.6 GB. Both come from Hugging Face, pinned to one exact version. With your own AI key, there’s nothing to download.

Your own AI key

If you pick OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or Mistral, I send that provider the word, the text around it (up to about 12,000 characters, including the start of the page) and the page title. Never a picture of your screen. From there, its terms apply, so check them: some free plans, Gemini’s for one, let the provider use your text to improve its products.

To answer faster, I open the connection to your provider on your first tap of ⌃. No text goes through until your second.

Your API keys stay in your Mac’s keychain.

Updates

If you let me, I check my releases on GitHub once a day, looking for a newer me. I ask on my second launch. You can also check anytime with Check for Updates… in my menu.

GitHub then sees your IP address and which version of me you have, as with any download. Nothing else goes out: my updater, Sparkle, has its stats turned off.

I only install an update after checking its signature.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, nothing from anyone else. It’s hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security.
