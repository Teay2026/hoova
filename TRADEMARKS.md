# Hoova's name, logo and mascot

Hoova's code is open source, under the MIT license ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). Its name, its logo, its mascot and its app icon are not. They belong to Youness Tahiri El Alaoui, all rights reserved.

The files that draw them say so at their top: "All rights reserved. Not covered by the MIT license." They are `Sources/Hoova/Mascot.swift` (the mascot, its poses, the logo and the menu bar icon) and the layers of the app icon, in `Resources/AppIcon.icon/`.

## What you can do

- Say that your project is based on Hoova, or that it works with Hoova.
- Show Hoova's name, logo or mascot to talk about Hoova itself: in an article, a review, a video or a talk.

## What you can't do without written permission

- Use the name "Hoova", the logo, the mascot or the icon for your own app, fork, service or merchandise.
- Make something look like it comes from Hoova or from its author.

## If you publish a fork

Give it another name and another icon. Keep the MIT notice from [LICENSE](LICENSE), as the license asks.
